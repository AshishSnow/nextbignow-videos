Okay, so what is a sneeze, really? Something tickles the inside of your nose. A nerve fires off a message to your brain, and your brain basically yells: everybody out!
Your eyes snap shut. You suck in a big breath. Then your chest and belly muscles slam it out through your nose and mouth. And once it starts, you can't stop it.
You've probably heard a sneeze shoots out at a hundred and sixty kilometers per hour. [sarcastic] Yeah, no. When scientists filmed real sneezes with super fast cameras, the top speed was about sixteen. That's a jogger, not a race car.

And whatever you do, don't hold one in. In two thousand twenty-three, a man in Scotland was driving when a sneeze hit. So he pinched his nose and clamped his mouth shut.
