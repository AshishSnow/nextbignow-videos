She fell two miles out of the sky... and lived.
And what saved her? One sentence her dad once told her. If you're ever lost, find water, and follow it.
Christmas Eve, nineteen seventy-one. Seventeen-year-old Juliane Koepcke is flying over the Amazon when lightning hits her plane. It breaks apart in the air. She falls ten thousand feet, still strapped to her seat.
She wakes up alone in the jungle. A broken collarbone. One shoe. A little bag of candy.
So she finds a stream... and follows it. For ten days. Her wounds fill with maggots.
On day ten, she finds a boat and an empty hut. The next day, men come back... and find her.
Ninety-two people were on that plane. She was the only survivor.
Would you have made it out?
