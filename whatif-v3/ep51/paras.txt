On November fifth, Wall Street lost trillions of dollars in a single day. And the reason fits in two letters. A.I. So what happens if the AI bubble pops?
---
Here's what happened. The S&P 500 dropped four point four percent, its worst day since the tariff crash of twenty twenty-five. The Nasdaq fell five and a half percent. AMD dropped seventeen percent. Nvidia, the king of AI chips, fell nine. Investors got scared that the giant wave of AI data centers could slow down, because borrowing got more expensive, and voters are getting angry about power bills.
---
And here's why people keep saying the word bubble. Look at how the money moves. In twenty twenty-five, Nvidia said it would invest up to a hundred billion dollars in OpenAI. And OpenAI buys Nvidia chips. AMD gave OpenAI the right to own up to ten percent of AMD. And OpenAI buys AMD chips. Some of the money is basically going around in a circle.
---
We've seen this movie before. In March two thousand, the internet was the future, and everyone knew it. Cisco, the company that made the internet's routers, became the most valuable company on Earth. Then the bubble popped. Over the next two and a half years, the Nasdaq lost seventy-eight percent. Cisco lost about eighty-six. And Pets dot com went from a Super Bowl ad to shutting down in less than a year.
---
But here's the part people forget. The internet didn't die. Amazon's stock fell more than ninety percent, and it still became one of the biggest companies in history. The bubble popped. The technology won.
---
So here's my prediction. AI is real, just like the internet was real. But not every company riding it will survive. If money gets tighter, the ones borrowing billions to build data centers get hit first. Prices fall, some names disappear, and a few winners come out way bigger. And no, this isn't financial advice.
