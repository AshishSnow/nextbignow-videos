# Topic research — 28 Nov 2026 (what topics work on YouTube)

## Rules from data
1. TOPIC > packaging. Topics already proven on 2+ independent channels (same niche, <2 yrs) became 3x+ breakouts 47.5% vs 27.8% (OverseerOS, 1,345 videos). Before picking: find the subject winning on several channels, then do our own angle.
2. Ceiling by family (million-view long-form since 2023): crime/mystery median 4.5M (24% >10M); challenges/experiments 3.5M; education 2.1M (most channels, low ceiling); money/business lowest (0.9% >10M).
3. Outlier hit rate Aug 2026 (10x channel avg): personal finance 16.7%, true crime/unsolved 12.5%, AI tools 10%.
4. Titles: negative/conflict framing ~+22% views; short titles best; neutral info titles underperform (1of10, 300k videos). Within-channel title length/digits not significant (OverseerOS) — optimise the promise.
5. Shorts: 61% views from feed; seed audience test -> push; track Viewed vs Swiped; 13 s and ~60 s lengths did best (Nate Black 35B views); keep audience consistent; ~5 Shorts/month correlated with +46% long-form views.
6. Long-form: views rise to ~18-24 min; money topics 8-15 min sweet spot. Viral Short != long-form demand; test few long videos first.
7. Zack D. Films (biggest facts-Shorts channel, ~675M weekly views): body/medical "how it works", everyday mechanics, disasters, what-ifs; visual hook, calm VO + intense visuals, loops, no intro.
