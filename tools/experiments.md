# NextBigNow experiment log (framework v4, section 8.6)

Columns:
- **Ep**: episode number.
- **Topic**: the subject.
- **Format**: news, explainer, scenario or survival.
- **Text hook (frame 1)**: the title on the first frame.
- **Spoken hook**: line 1 of the voiceover.
- **Visual hook**: what moves in frame 1.
- **Changed brick + hypothesis**: the one brick swapped on purpose and the expected effect.
- **3s %**: share of viewers still watching after 3 seconds (Metricool IGRE28).
- **Avg watch**: average watch time (Metricool IGRE24).
- **Shares/Saves**: shares and saves (Metricool IGRE21 and IGRE12).
- **Verdict**: keep or drop the brick.
