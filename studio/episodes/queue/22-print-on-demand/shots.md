brand logos even as the "don't" examples — draw a generic guitar, a blank cartoon blob with a red "?", a plain "LOGO" circle; no income promise

Fresh-eyes review 11 Nov: the hook asks what the seller *keeps* (the $11.69 buys a product, it isn't a skim — print companies and shops are likely sponsors, keep them neutral); the $5.73 chip says "before ads, refunds, tax and your time".
