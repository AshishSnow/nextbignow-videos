| c2 | Refund within seven working days; gift cards don't count | ✅ 16 CFR 435.1 (definition of "prompt refund") + FTC guide | "within seven (7) working days" (credit sales where the seller is the creditor: "within one (1) billing cycle"). | d2, row 13 | The person taking the order is the seller, not whoever ships it | ✅ FTC guide | "the person soliciting the order, not the agent fulfilling it, is the seller under the Rule" (FTC guidance on drop shippers / fulfillment houses; the CFR itself has no "seller" definition, so say "its guide says"). |
