| i1, i2, e1 | The US can't tax exports; it's in the Constitution | US Constitution Art. I, §9, cl. 5: "No Tax or Duty shall be laid on Articles exported from any State." Cornell LII / CRS Constitution Annotated, "Export Clause and Taxes" (law.cornell.edu/constitution-conan/article-1/section-9/clause-5/export-clause-and-taxes): bars Congress from taxing exported articles; e.g. *United States v. IBM*, 517 U.S. 843 (1996) struck down a tax on insurance for exported goods; *U.S. Shoe* (1998) struck an export-cargo tax. Limits: user fees, general income tax on an exporter's income are allowed (so "export tax" only, never "no tax at all"). |
