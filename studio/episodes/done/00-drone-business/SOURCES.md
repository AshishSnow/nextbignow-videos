## Re-verified 1 Nov 2026 (episode run, research subagent + official pages)
- FAA Become a Drone Pilot (updated 9 Jun 2026): 16+, FAA-approved Knowledge Testing Center, recurrent every 24 calendar months (ALC-677, no cost). 60 questions / 70 % (FAA testing matrix via rotatepilot, Sep–Oct 2026; image-based chart questions since 26 Oct 2026). Fee $175 (rotatepilot, Sep 2026 — PSI page not reachable).
- FAA registration $5 / 3 years; Remote ID "like a digital license plate" (FAA newsroom 15 Mar 2024). Fines up to $75,000 per violation (FAA newsroom 6 Feb 2026).
- FCC fact sheet DOC-416839A1 (22 Dec 2025): new foreign UAS models can't get authorization; existing models' import/sale/use not prohibited. Exemptions (DA-26-875, 21 Aug 2026) don't cover DJI; DA-26-742 (17 Jul 2026) proposal not adopted; DJI's Ninth Circuit challenge pending (FCC motion to dismiss 16 Oct 2026). → VO changed to "new DJI models can't get FCC approval right now. Drones you already own still fly."
- Insurance: $1M liability typically ~$500–1,200/yr (Unmanned Aerial Online, 22 Aug 2026).
- Rates: TheDroneU (30 May 2026) $300 real-estate / $250 roof are "illustrative"; SkyeBrowse (16 Mar 2026) $150–250 entry real estate, $150–400 roof → VO changed to "rates vary by city… often pays a few hundred dollars"; screen shows $150–300 / $150–400.
- Airspace: FAA B4UFLY (check where you can fly) vs LAANC apps (controlled-airspace approval) → VO "the FAA's airspace app", chip "FAA B4UFLY app · LAANC approval apps".
- UK: CAA Operator ID £12.34 / 1 year + free Flyer ID test; EU: national operator registration + online A1/A3 test (no single EU fee quoted).
