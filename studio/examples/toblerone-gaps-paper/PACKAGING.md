# PACKAGING
Type: quip
Idea: Toblerone shrank its bars by widening the gaps, not raising the price.
Title: Toblerone Made the Gaps Bigger
Cover line: THE GAPS GOT BIGGER
Reward: Toblerone fixed rising costs by making the gaps bigger.
Gap: How much did you lose?
Payoff / last line: So check the weight, not the price.
Send line: Send this to the friend who always buys the big bar.
Sources: Fortune (8 Nov 2016): UK 170g -> 150g, 400g -> 360g, wider gaps, Mondelez Facebook post "higher costs for numerous ingredients"; Irish Times (8 Nov 2016): price and packaging kept the same, rising ingredient costs.
