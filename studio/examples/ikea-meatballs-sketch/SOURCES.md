# Sources: IKEA meatballs

- IKEA Museum, "The world's biggest restaurant": https://www.ikeamuseum.com/en/explore/the-story-of-ikea/the-worlds-biggest-restaurant/
  - After the 1953 exhibition in Älmhult: "Ingvar Kamprad had made an important discovery: visitors with full stomachs stay longer and buy more."
  - Kamprad aimed for a food profit margin of about 5% and wanted it below 10%.
  - First real restaurant (IKEA Baren) opened 1960.
- Ingka Group newsroom, "Plenty of food for thought at IKEA" (3 Feb 2020): "One billion meatballs are sold and served in IKEA stores globally every year."
