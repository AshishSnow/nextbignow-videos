YT TITLE: IKEA Sells Cheap Meatballs So You'll Buy the Sofa

YT DESCRIPTION:
IKEA sells you cheap meatballs for one reason: so you'll buy the sofa. IKEA's own museum says founder Ingvar Kamprad made a discovery back in the 1950s: "visitors with full stomachs stay longer and buy more." So IKEA put food inside its stores, and people eat a ton: IKEA has said about a billion meatballs a year. The twist? Kamprad didn't want the food to make much money. He aimed for about a 5% profit.

So the meatballs aren't the business. They're the reason you stay for the sofa.

Meatballs every trip, or straight to the sofas?

Sources: IKEA Museum ("The world's biggest restaurant"); Ingka Group newsroom (Feb 2020).
