# Domino's "like cardboard" — Short (9:16, ~65 s), vintage comic, black + yellow
1. Would you put your own customers on TV saying your pizza tastes like cardboard?
2. Domino's did. On purpose. And its sales jumped.
3. Twenty oh eight. Its American stores are selling about five percent less than the year before.
4. So it asks real customers. One says the crust is like cardboard. Another says the sauce tastes like ketchup.
5. Most companies would bury that. Domino's fixed the pizza instead.
6. Domino's says the testing took about two years. Dozens of cheeses, fifteen sauces, nearly fifty crust seasonings.
7. Then the scary part. In December twenty oh nine, the new ads play those complaints right on TV.
8. The boss of its American stores, Patrick Doyle, says: there comes a time when you know you've got to make a change.
9. Did it work? January to March, twenty ten: sales at its American stores, up over fourteen percent. A year earlier? Up just one.
10. Doyle credited a better pizza, and the honesty.
11. So here's the move: read your worst reviews. If they're right, fix it. Then say so out loud.
12. Your product gets slammed online. Air it, or hide it? Tell me in the comments.
