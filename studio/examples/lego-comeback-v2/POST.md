YT TITLE: Lego Almost Went Broke. Then It Did Less.

YT DESCRIPTION:
In 2004 Lego posted the biggest loss in its history and nearly went broke. It had theme parks, video games, even clothes, and sales fell 25% in 2003. So it cut costs by a fifth, sold all four Legoland parks, and went back to the brick. Profitable again in 2005. In 2025: about $2.5 billion in profit and sales growing more than twice as fast as the toy market.

What would you cut to save your business? Tell me in the comments.
