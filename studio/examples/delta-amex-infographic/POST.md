YT TITLE: Delta's Biggest Paycheck Isn't Tickets. It's a Credit Card.

YT DESCRIPTION:
Delta flew more than 200 million people last year. But one of its biggest paychecks didn't come from a seat. It came from a credit card.

Every time you swipe a Delta card from American Express, you earn miles, and American Express pays Delta for those miles. In 2025, Delta says that added up to $8.2 billion. Delta's whole operating profit? $5.8 billion.

So Delta isn't really an airline with a credit card. It's a credit card with wings.

Do you have an airline credit card?

Sources: Delta Air Lines full-year 2025 results; Fortune (Apr 2026); Payments Dive.
