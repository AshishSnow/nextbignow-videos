YT TITLE: HP Makes 9x More From Your Ink Than Your Printer (Its Own Numbers)

YT DESCRIPTION:
HP makes way more money from your ink than from your printer. In fiscal 2025, HP sold about $1.2 billion of home printers, but almost $11 billion of ink and toner (supplies), over nine times more (HP 10-K).

Why would HP care what you print? Its CEO told CNBC: "Every time a customer buys a printer, it's an investment for us." And if a customer "doesn't print enough or doesn't use our supplies, it's a bad investment."

It's like a razor. The handle is cheap. The blades make the money. So the printer is the bait. The ink is the business.

Do you buy the brand ink, or the cheap stuff?

Sources: HP Inc. Form 10-K FY2025; The Register (Jan 2024) on the CNBC interview.
