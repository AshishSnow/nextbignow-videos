YT TITLE: Disney Makes 2x More From Parks Than Movies (Its Own Numbers)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Disney makes more money from its theme parks than from its movies. Way more. In fiscal 2025, Disney's Experiences segment (parks, cruises and more) made almost $10 billion in operating income. Entertainment (movies, TV and streaming)? About $4.7 billion.

Why? A movie ticket is one payment. A park day is tickets, hotels, food, toys and that photo you didn't need. It's like your favorite band: the songs get you hooked, the concert and the T-shirt pay the bills.

So the movies sell you the dream. The parks cash it in.

Movie night or park day, which would you rather spend on?

Source: The Walt Disney Company FY2025 earnings release (Nov 2025).
