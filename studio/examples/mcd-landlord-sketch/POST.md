YT TITLE: McDonald's Makes More From Rent Than Burgers (Its Own Report Proves It)

YT DESCRIPTION:
McDonald's makes more money from rent than from selling its own burgers. Its 2025 annual report shows over $10.4 billion in rent, vs about $9.7 billion in sales from the restaurants it runs itself.

Who pays the rent? Franchise owners, who run about 95% of McDonald's restaurants. McDonald's usually owns or leases the land and the building, rents it to them, and takes a cut of every sale. Its own restaurants keep about 15 cents per dollar as margin. Franchise money keeps about 84.

So McDonald's sells burgers. But it makes its money on the building.

Would you rather own the restaurant, or the land under it?

Source: McDonald's Corporation Form 10-K, fiscal 2025 (margins are our math from its reported figures).
