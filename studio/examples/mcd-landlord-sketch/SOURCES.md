# Sources: McDonald's is a landlord

McDonald's Corporation Form 10-K for fiscal 2025 (year ended 31 Dec 2025), as filed (copy: https://www.lobbyregister.bundestag.de/media/6a/f9/769258/10-k-2025.pdf ; also SEC EDGAR):
- Revenues from franchised restaurants 2025: Rents $10,442M; Royalties $6,018M; Initial fees $88M; total $16,548M.
- Sales by Company-owned and operated restaurants 2025: $9,690M ("about nine point seven billion in sales").
- Company-owned and operated margins $1,422M (~15% of company-operated sales, our arithmetic).
- Franchised margins $13,930M (~84% of franchised revenues, our arithmetic).
- "Approximately 95%" of 45,356 restaurants franchised at year-end 2025.
- "the Company generally owns or secures a long-term lease on the land and building for the restaurant location"; franchisees pay "rent and royalties based upon a percent of sales".
- Results release: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/Q4-full-year-2025-results.html
- "Rent beat burgers" compares rent revenue with company-operated restaurant sales (both revenue lines in the 10-K). "The real business is the building" is our framing.
