YT TITLE: Who Owns Rolex? Not a Billionaire. A Charity.

YT DESCRIPTION:
Rolex sells around 11 billion Swiss francs of watches a year (2025 estimate). But you can't buy a single share of it. No stock. No owning family.

In 1944, founder Hans Wilsdorf lost his wife. The next year, he set up a foundation. When he died in 1960, he left it all his Rolex shares. Today the Hans Wilsdorf Foundation owns Rolex, and it funds scholarships, the arts and people in need.

So the owner of the world's most famous watch brand isn't a billionaire. It's a charity.
