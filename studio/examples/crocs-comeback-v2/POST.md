YT TITLE: How Crocs Came Back From $1 (The Ugliest Shoe Ever)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Crocs stock went from almost $70 to about $1 in a year, and the company lost $185 million in 2008. Then Crocs stopped hiding the "ugly" clog and went all in on it, plus little charms called Jibbitz that pulled in about $271 million in 2024 alone. Last year the Crocs brand did $3.3 billion in sales. Wild comeback.
