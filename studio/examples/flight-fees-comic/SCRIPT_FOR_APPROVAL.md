# "$19 flight... $118 at the gate" — Short (~70 s), comic, black + yellow, IG + YT cuts
1. I booked a nineteen-dollar flight. At the gate, I paid ninety-nine dollars more. For one bag.
2. Here's the trap. On Frontier, a carry-on can cost twenty-nine dollars if you add it when you book. Wait until the gate? Ninety-nine to a hundred and fifteen. Same bag. Same plane.
3. And the person at the gate? A 2024 US Senate report found Frontier paid staff up to ten dollars for every bag they made a passenger check at the gate.
4. Trap two: the seat. Frontier's priciest extra-legroom seat went from fifty dollars in 2018 to a hundred and forty-one in 2023.
5. Trap three, in Europe. Forget to check in online with Ryanair, and the airport desk charges fifty-five pounds.
6. So how does a cheap ticket make money? Ryanair's average fare last year was about fifty-one euros. Then each passenger added about twenty-four euros in extras. That's almost five billion euros a year.
7. The ticket gets you on board. The extras pay the bills.
8. So: add your bag when you book, check in online, and measure your bag at home.
