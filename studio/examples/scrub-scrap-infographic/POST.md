YT TITLE: 3M Left This Foam Behind. It Became a $220M Sponge.

YT DESCRIPTION:
3M bought his company, but it left one thing behind: a foam he kept in a box he called scrap.

Aaron Krause made a foam hand scrubber, and body shops wouldn't buy it. So it sat in that box for years. Then one fall day in 2011, he grabbed it to scrub his lawn furniture. Cold water made it firm. Warm water made it soft. He gave it a smile-shaped hole for cleaning utensils and called it Scrub Daddy.

Did it work? On Shark Tank, Lori Greiner put in $200,000. And in 2023, sales topped $220 million (reported).

So the thing 3M left behind? That was the real business.
