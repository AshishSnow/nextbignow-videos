# Sources: Santa's red suit

- The Coca-Cola Company, "Haddon Sundblom and the Coca-Cola Santas": https://www.coca-colacompany.com/about-us/history/haddon-sundblom-and-the-coca-cola-santas
  - "Santa appeared in a red coat before Sundblom painted him." (quoted in the script as "Its words")
  - "From 1931 to 1964, Coca-Cola advertising showed Santa delivering toys."; paintings adapted for "magazines and on store displays, billboards, posters, calendars and plush dolls".
- Coca-Cola US FAQ, "Did Coca-Cola create Santa Claus?": "Coca-Cola did not create the legend of Santa Claus."; Sundblom commissioned in 1931.
  https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/about-us/faq/did-coca-cola-create-santa-claus
- Snopes: such images were "common long before Coca-Cola's first Sundblom-drawn Santa Claus advertisement appeared in 1931"; "at best what Coca-Cola popularized was an image they borrowed, not one they created." https://snopes.com/cokelore/santa.asp
- "Like a song famous from a cover" and "it made him famous" are our framing.
