YT TITLE: Coca-Cola Didn't Give Santa His Red Suit (Coke's Own Website Says So)

YT DESCRIPTION:
You've heard Coca-Cola gave Santa his red suit. But Coke's own website says no. In its words: "Santa appeared in a red coat before Sundblom painted him." Haddon Sundblom was the artist Coke hired in 1931 to paint Santa for its ads.

So why does everyone believe the myth? Because from 1931 to 1964, Coke's Santa showed up in magazines, on billboards and in store displays every Christmas. It's like a song that gets famous from a cover: Coke didn't write it, it just played it everywhere.

So Coke didn't make Santa red. It made him famous.

Did you believe the Coke myth?
