YT TITLE: Where Your Returns REALLY Go (Mystery Pallets, Explained)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Americans are sending back almost $850 billion of stuff this year, about 16% of everything bought, and 19% of online orders (NRF). You drop the box and you're done, but for the store that's where it gets expensive: shipping it back, opening it, checking it, repacking it. Optoro says processing a return can cost up to 66% of the item's price. So lots of returns get sold off by the pallet to liquidators, who resell them online or to bargain stores. And the wild part? "90% of the time, there's nothing wrong with it," a returns industry leader told CNBC. What doesn't sell can end up in landfills, up to 5.8 billion pounds a year by Optoro's estimate.

Would you buy a mystery pallet? Yes or no in the comments.

Sources: NRF / Happy Returns 2025 Retail Returns Landscape (Oct 2025); CNBC (Feb 2022) with Optoro and Reverse Logistics Association.
