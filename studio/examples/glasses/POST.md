Your sunglasses, the shop that sold them and your eye insurance can all lead back to one company: EssilorLuxottica.

It owns Ray-Ban, Oakley, Persol and Oliver Peoples, and makes glasses under licence for Chanel, Prada, Armani, Versace and Michael Kors. In 2018 Luxottica combined with Essilor, the lens maker behind Varilux and Transitions.

It runs Sunglass Hut, LensCrafters and Target Optical: 19,709 stores by 30 September 2026. In the US it also owns EyeMed, a vision benefits plan with tens of millions of members.

In 2001, months after Luxottica bought Sunglass Hut, Oakley learned the chain would order far fewer of its glasses, and Oakley's stock fell 35% in a day. In 2007 Luxottica bought Oakley for $2.1 billion. In 2025 Meta, which makes smart glasses with Ray-Ban, bought about 3% of the company.
