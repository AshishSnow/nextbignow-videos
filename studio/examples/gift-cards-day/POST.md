YT TITLE: Stores Make Millions From Gift Cards You Never Use (It's Called Breakage)

YT DESCRIPTION:
43% of Americans have at least one gift card they haven't used, and the average is $244 (Bankrate). When you buy a gift card, the store already has your cash, but it can't count it as a sale until somebody spends it. When a company decides a card probably won't get used, it can book that money as revenue. It's called breakage. Starbucks booked about $222 million of it in fiscal 2025. And 1 in 5 Americans say they've let a card expire.

How much is sitting in your drawer? Drop it in the comments.

Sources: Bankrate gift card survey (Sep 2024); Starbucks 10-K for fiscal 2025.
