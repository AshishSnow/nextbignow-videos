YT TITLE: The Sneaker Maker You've Never Heard Of (Makes Shoes for Nike AND Adidas)

YT DESCRIPTION:
We all know Nike, Adidas, New Balance and Puma. But have you heard of Yue Yuen? It has made shoes for every one of them.

In 2025, Yue Yuen shipped 252.2 million pairs of shoes. That's about 8 pairs every single second. And it got about $21 per pair, on average. The brands do the design and the ads. Yue Yuen does the building.

So the big sneaker war? Both sides buy their boots from the same guy.

Which brand did you think made its own shoes?

Sources: Yue Yuen 2025 annual results; World Footwear; Footwear News; Business of Fashion.
