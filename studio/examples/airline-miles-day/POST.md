YT TITLE: Your Airline Barely Makes Money Flying You (A Credit Card Made Delta $8.2B)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Your airline barely makes money flying you. The real money is the card in your wallet. In 2025, American Express paid Delta about $8.2 billion. Delta's whole adjusted profit before taxes for the year? About $5 billion. So one credit card deal beat the airline's entire profit.

Why? Because miles are money. Amex pays Delta for its SkyMiles, then hands them to you every time you swipe. Coffee, groceries, gas: you're nowhere near a plane, and Delta still gets paid. And Delta's CEO says he expects about $9 billion from the deal this year.

So is Delta an airline? Sure. It's also a rewards program with planes attached.

Sources: Delta full-year 2025 results (Jan 2026); Payments Dive (Jul 2026).
