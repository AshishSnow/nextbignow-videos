YT TITLE: Your Grocery Store Makes Just 2 Cents on Your Food (Here's the Real Money)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Your grocery store keeps about 2 cents of every dollar you spend (FMI, 2025). On a $100 cart that's about two bucks, and it still has to pay for the lights, the staff, the freezers. So where's the real money? The screen at checkout. The coupon in your app. The "sponsored" product at the top of your search. Those are ads, and brands pay the store to put them in front of you. Walmart's ad business brought in nearly $6.4 billion last fiscal year, plus over $4.3 billion in membership fees. You came in for milk. The store came in for your attention.

Do you ever tap the sponsored stuff? Yes or no in the comments.

Sources: FMI (Jul 2026); Walmart fiscal 2026 results via PPC Land and Digital Commerce 360 (Feb 2026).
