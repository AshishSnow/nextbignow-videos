# Sources: movie theater popcorn

All figures from AMC Entertainment Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025 (year ended 31 Dec 2025), filed with the SEC:
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157926000016/amc-20251231x10k.htm

- U.S. markets film exhibition costs = 51.2% of admissions revenue ("about half of every U.S. ticket dollar went to the studios").
- Consolidated average ticket price $12.09 ("a twelve-dollar ticket" -> about $6 to the studios is our worked example).
- U.S. markets food and beverage costs = 18.1% of food and beverage revenue ("about eighteen cents"; 82 cents left before staff and rent is our arithmetic).
- U.S. markets food and beverage revenue per patron $8.57.
- U.S. markets food and beverage revenue $1,335.5 million ("over one point three billion dollars in one year").
- 10-K business section: "Food and beverage sales are our second largest source of revenue after box office admissions."
- "A snack bar with a really big screen" is our framing, not a quote.
