YT TITLE: Your Movie Theater Barely Makes Money on Your Ticket (The Real Money Is Popcorn)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Your movie theater barely makes money on your ticket. AMC's 2025 annual report says about half of every U.S. ticket dollar went to the studios. The real money is the snack counter: for every dollar AMC made on food and drinks in the U.S., the food itself cost about 18 cents. The average AMC moviegoer in the U.S. spent about $8.57 on snacks, and that adds up to over $1.3 billion in one year.

So a movie theater is really a snack bar with a really big screen.

Do you buy the popcorn every time? Yes or no, in the comments.

Source: AMC Entertainment Holdings Form 10-K, fiscal year 2025 (SEC).
