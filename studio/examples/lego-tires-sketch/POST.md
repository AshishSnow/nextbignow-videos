YT TITLE: The World's Biggest Tire Maker Isn't Michelin. It's LEGO.

YT DESCRIPTION:
The company that makes the most tires on Earth isn't Michelin. It's LEGO. Guinness World Records says LEGO made 381 million tires in a single year (2010), and the European Rubber Journal says that's the most tire units of any maker.

The catch? They're toy tires: tiny rubber rings for tiny plastic trucks. It's like the most-read writer on the planet being the person who writes cereal boxes.

So the world's biggest tire maker has never made a tire you could drive on.

Did you know this one?

Sources: Guinness World Records; European Rubber Journal (Jun 2012).
