YT TITLE: Amazon Barely Makes Its Money Selling You Stuff (AWS = 57% of Profit)
YT DESCRIPTION:
Amazon barely makes its money selling you stuff. The real money is the cloud. In 2025, all of Amazon made about $80 billion in operating income, and AWS alone made about $45.6 billion of it, more than half. But AWS was less than a fifth of Amazon's sales. So the boxes bring the crowds. The servers bring the money.

It's like a mall that makes most of its money from the parking garage you never think about. So Amazon isn't really a store with a cloud. It's a cloud with a store.

Did you know AWS was this big?

Source: Amazon.com Q4 and full-year 2025 results (Feb 2026).
