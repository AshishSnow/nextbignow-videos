YT TITLE: Chick-fil-A Closes Sundays and Still Sells ~2x a McDonald's (Per Store)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Chick-fil-A closes every Sunday. But each store still sells almost double what a McDonald's does. The average U.S. Chick-fil-A did about $7.7 million in 2025. The average McDonald's? About $4.1 million, and McDonald's is open seven days (QSR Magazine, year-end 2025 U.S. stores).

Has the boss ever added up what Sundays cost? CEO Andrew Cathy told Fortune: "I never have because it's never something that we're entertaining."

So Chick-fil-A takes Sundays off, and still beats McDonald's store for store, in six days, not seven.

Would you open on Sundays if you ran it?

Sources: QSR Magazine (2025 AUVs); Fortune (Oct 2026); Restaurant Business (2025 FDD).
