YT TITLE: Black Friday Wasn't Named for Store Profits (Cops Named It)

YT DESCRIPTION:
You've heard Black Friday means stores finally go "in the black." But that's not where the name came from. In the 1950s, Philadelphia police used "Black Friday" for the chaos the day after Thanksgiving: huge crowds of shoppers, plus fans in town for the Army-Navy game, traffic jams and extra-long shifts. Stores hated the name and tried to rebrand it "Big Friday." It flopped. The red-ink-to-black-ink profit story only caught on in the late 1980s. Last Thanksgiving weekend, a record 202.9 million Americans shopped (NRF).

So if you're stuck in Black Friday traffic, congrats. You're living the original meaning.
