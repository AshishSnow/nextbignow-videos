YT TITLE: Costco's $4.99 Chicken Costs It Millions. On Purpose.

YT DESCRIPTION:
Costco's rotisserie chicken has cost $4.99 since 2009. In 2015 its CFO said Costco was willing to give up $30-40 million a year in gross margin to keep that price. Then it built its own $450 million chicken plant in Nebraska. In 2025 it sold 157 million of them. Here's the bet behind that $4.99 price.
