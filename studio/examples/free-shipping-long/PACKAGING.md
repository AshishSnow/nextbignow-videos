Sources (2 per fact; single-source items are attributed on air):
- Free-shipping minimum → shoppers spent 9.4% more, overshoot, fewer orders (-6.4%): HBS Working Knowledge on Ngwe & Chen, "Shipping Fees and Product Assortment in Online Retail" (Zalora, 2M+ orders) — attributed to Harvard Business School research.
- Amazon non-Prime minimum $25 → $35 (NPR, Tom's Hardware, 2013); $35 → $49 (CBS News, CNN Money, Feb 2016), framed as a push into Prime ($99/yr then).
- Prime members ~$1,300/yr vs ~$700 non-members (CIRP 2017 via Fortune; Yahoo/BI "nearly double").
- BNPL: up to 14% revenue lift, >2/3 of BNPL volume net-new sales (Stripe test, 150K+ sessions); merchant fees up to ~3x a credit card transaction (Payments Dive).
- LendingTree 2022 survey (1,537): ~70% of BNPL users spend more than if paying upfront, 42% paid late, 23% regretted — attributed. Federal Reserve FEDS note (Dec 2024): 55% of users said BNPL was the "only way I could afford it".
