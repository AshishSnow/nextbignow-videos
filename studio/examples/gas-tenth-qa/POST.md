YT TITLE: Why Is Gas Always $2.99 and 9/10?

YT DESCRIPTION:
Why is gas always $2.99 and 9/10? Short answer: it makes the price look cheaper, and it pays the station. It started in the 1930s, when gas taxes came in fractions of a cent. Then stations noticed drivers on the highway read the first digits, so $2.99 9/10 looks way cheaper than $3.00. And that tiny fraction isn't tiny: stations make about 10-15 cents a gallon on gas, so the 9/10 can be up to 10% of their gas profit (NACS). One owner who dropped it in 2006 lost about $23 a day.

Did you ever notice it? Tell me in the comments.

Sources: NACS (convenience store industry), Mental Floss.
