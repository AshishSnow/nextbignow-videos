YT TITLE: Netflix's CEO Said Its Real Competition Is Your Sleep ("And We're Winning")

YT DESCRIPTION:
Netflix's boss once said his real competition isn't other streamers. It's your sleep. Reed Hastings: "You get a show or a movie you're really dying to watch, and you end up staying up late at night, so we actually compete with sleep." Then he added: "And we're winning!"

Why would sleep be the enemy? Because every hour you sleep is an hour you're not watching. It's like an all-you-can-eat buffet that never closes: the only thing that stops you is you.

So when the next episode starts in five seconds, that's not a timer. That's the competition.

Source: Fast Company (Nov 2017), Reed Hastings at Summit LA.
