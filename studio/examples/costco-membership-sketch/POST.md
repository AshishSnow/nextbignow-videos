YT TITLE: Costco Barely Makes Money on What It Sells You (The Card Is the Business)
YT DESCRIPTION:
Costco barely makes money on the stuff it sells you. The real money is your membership card. In fiscal 2025, Costco made about $8.1 billion in net profit, and membership fees alone brought in $5.3 billion, like two-thirds of the whole profit. To get there, Costco sold almost $270 billion of stuff, keeping prices low on purpose.

It's like a gym that sells cheap protein bars: the bars keep you coming back, the membership pays the bills. So Costco isn't really a store. It's a club that happens to sell hot dogs.

Is your Costco card worth it?

Source: Costco fiscal 2025 operating results (Sep 2025).
