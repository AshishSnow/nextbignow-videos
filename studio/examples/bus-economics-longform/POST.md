TITLE: The Economics of Owning a Bus Business (USA + Europe)

DESCRIPTION:
A new coach can cost $500,000. So who actually makes money from intercity buses: the owner, the driver, or the app? We break down what a coach costs, the 3 ways a bus earns, how FlixBus grew huge without owning buses, where every dollar goes (fuel, drivers, insurance, the loan), and the rules in the US and Europe.

Would you rather own the buses, or the app? Tell us in the comments.
