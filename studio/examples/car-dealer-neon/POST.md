YT TITLE: Your Car Dealer Barely Makes Money on Your Car (Here's Where It Really Comes From)

YT DESCRIPTION:
Your car dealer makes about 5% on a new car. On a $30K ride, that's around $1,500, and the showroom still has bills to pay. So where's the real money? First, the finance office: at Asbury, one of America's biggest dealer groups, that office was about 4% of sales but 23% of gross profit. Then the service bay: about 50% margin on service. US dealers did over $164 billion in service and parts last year. Plot twist: 42% of drivers now say a chain shop is their main service spot, up from 20% in 2020.

Dealer or the shop down the street? Where do you get yours serviced?

Sources: CNBC (Aug 2026) with Kerrigan Advisors and Ducker Carlisle data; Asbury Automotive (Jan-Jun 2026); NADA Data 2025.
