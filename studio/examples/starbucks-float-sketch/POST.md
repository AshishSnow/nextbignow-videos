YT TITLE: Starbucks Is Holding $1.75 Billion of Customers' Money (Its Own Report)
YT DESCRIPTION:
Starbucks is holding about $1.75 billion of its customers' money: balances loaded onto gift cards and the app (plus loyalty) that haven't been spent yet. Starbucks' own fiscal 2025 annual report lists $1,751.7 million for stored value cards and its loyalty program as of September 28, 2025.

The twist: you pay first and get your coffee later, so Starbucks holds your money in between. It's like paying for a pizza a week before they even make it.

So every time you top up the app, you're kind of lending Starbucks money. For free.

Source: Starbucks Corporation Form 10-K, fiscal 2025.
