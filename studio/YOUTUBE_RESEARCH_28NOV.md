## Rules that come out of it
1. FAMILIAR brand the viewer already has feelings about (nostalgic chains, food, retail, airlines). Obscure = dead.
2. ONE story, ONE decision, told in cause-and-effect order. Lists of 5 loosely linked tricks underperform ("covers 3-4 decisions thinly").
3. Proven angles: decline/what happened; how X really makes money; the rigged/secret business of X; X vs Y; why X can't survive.
4. Length that works for this niche: 9-12 min (1,400-1,800 words). Our 4-5 min is below the band.
5. No slow intro: prove the title in the first 30 s.
6. Policy (YouTube clarification 13 Jul 2026, Tubefilter): templated image slideshows / repetitive formats are "inauthentic" and not monetizable; AI voice itself is not banned; original commentary + educational value is the protection. → vary visuals per video, real analysis, our own take.
7. Algorithm notes (vendor blogs, weak evidence): satisfaction signals (surveys, return next day) weigh with watch time; chapters help.
