# NextBigNow / FreshNestHub short-script framework (from "EXACTLY How to Write INSANELY Good YouTube Scripts", owner-shared Nov 2026)

Inputs for every script (the 3 "groups"):
1. STYLE: our own best scripts (chai myth-bust, GTA 6 rich, smart glasses) = tone, pacing, American young-guy voice, short spoken lines.
2. PRINCIPLES: this file.
3. RAW MATERIAL: the real sources for the topic (numbers, names, dates checked).

Audience: curious US/global viewers, 18-35, phone-first. Simple words, talk to ONE person ("you").

HOOK (first 3-6 s):
- Line 1 = the title, confirms the click (same promise as thumbnail/title).
- Credibility/proof fast: a real number or who found it ("Scientists bought 212...").
- A twist they haven't heard.
- Why it matters to THEM (your phone, your money, your food).
- Plant a seed: hint a payoff still coming ("here's the part nobody's posting").

BODY:
- Strongest point first. Then reveal points one at a time (never all at once).
- Each point: context -> what it means for you -> tie back to the big picture.
- Curiosity gaps: answer one question, open the next.
- Rehook every ~12-15 s in a Short (every 30-40 s long-form): "But here's the twist...", "And that's just the beginning", "So what happens next?"
- Emotional waves: shock -> relief -> worry -> humor. Zoom into a detail, zoom out to the big picture.
- Pattern break each section: new visual, a question, a stat, a joke.

OUTRO:
- One-line takeaway (what they now know).
- Visualize the outcome ("you don't have to quit your oat latte, just mix it up").
- Name the next roadblock / open question, then the action: comment YES/NO (IG: send to a friend; YT: subscribe for what happens next).

Avoid: flat lecture, generic AI phrasing, dumping every point at once, drifting from the title promise.
