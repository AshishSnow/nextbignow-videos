- 2026-11-08 | THE MORPH CUT (editing / transitions) | Morph cuts hide a seam by matching 'position in frame, and matching direction of motion'; it's 'more important to match the momentum of the motion than find a perfect match pose'; opposite motion breaks it even in the identical position; '8-10 frames of cross dissolve' work best (the half-second-to-a-second default is too long); put the seam where there is a pause (D.
