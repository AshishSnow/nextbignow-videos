1. Red lipstick and candy colour made from crushed insects (carmine / cochineal, E120; FDA requires "carmine" on labels).
2. Shimmer in some cosmetics comes from fish scales (guanine / "pearl essence").
3. A fungus (Malassezia) eats your scalp oil — the main driver of dandruff.
4. Your tattoo is held in place by immune cells that eat the ink and pass it on when they die (2018 J Exp Med mouse study).
5. Why fingers wrinkle in water — a nerve-controlled reflex, not water soaking in (grip hypothesis).
6. Grey hair: pigment stem cells get "stuck" and stop working (NYU, Nature 2023).
7. Goosebumps: tiny muscles at each hair follicle, a leftover from furry ancestors.

9. Snail slime (snail mucin) in skincare — what it is and what studies actually show.
