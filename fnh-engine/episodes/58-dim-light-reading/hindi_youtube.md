Topic: कम रोशनी में पढ़ने से आँखें खराब होती हैं? (same verified facts as the English ep 58)
Facts: Johns Hopkins Medicine 'Six Children's Eye Health Misconceptions Demystified' (Funari & Min, Jun 2026): dim light makes the eyes work harder to focus ->
strain, headache, fatigue, blur, 'short term and temporary', gone after rest; 'no evidence that dim light causes permanent damage'; myopia progression = less outdoor
time + shorter reading distance, not dim light. Univ. of Utah Health (Moran Eye Center, 2021): 'reading in dim light will not hurt your eyes'. WebMD archive (Dr R. Gans,
Cleveland Clinic Cole Eye Institute): no long-term harm; short-term dryness because you blink less. American Academy of Ophthalmology: myopia = the eye grows too long;
1-2 h outdoors a day; mechanism unclear (one idea: daylight raises dopamine in the eye). US National Academies (2024): 1-2 h outdoors a day for children;
'unequivocal evidence that increased time outdoors slows excessive eye growth'.
Spoken length target: ~40-45 s (≈ 110 words). Payoff (डॉक्टर: नहीं) at ~15 %, the real risk (धूप की कमी) at ~50 %.
