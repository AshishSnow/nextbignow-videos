Topic: उंगलियाँ चटकाने से गठिया (arthritis) होता है? (same verified facts as the English ep 57)
Facts: Cleveland Clinic 'Is cracking your knuckles bad for you?' (Dr Wesley Baker, 21 Sep 2026): won't cause arthritis; gas in the joint fluid; a 2015 study: the sound is a
gas-filled cavity FORMING; see a provider for swelling / crooked fingers. Harvard Health: probably won't raise arthritis risk. Kawchuk et al., PLOS ONE 2015 (Univ. of Alberta): real-time
MRI, cracking = the cavity forming; ~20 min refractory. Cedars-Sinai (Dr R. Klapper, 2018): does not lead to arthritis; ~20 min before it cracks again; pain / swelling / limited motion =
signs of damage. Unger, Arthritis & Rheumatism 1998 (via Scientific American, Dec 2009): left hand cracked at least twice a day for 50 years, right hand as control: no arthritis in either hand.
Spoken length target: ~40-45 s (≈ 100 words). Payoff (डॉक्टर: नहीं) at ~15 %, the 'pop' explained by ~45 %.
