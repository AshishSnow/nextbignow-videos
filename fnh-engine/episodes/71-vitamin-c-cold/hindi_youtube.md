# Ep 71 — Hindi YouTube Short (Bunty voice, ElevenLabs v3) — "क्या विटामिन C से ज़ुकाम रुकता है?"
Same verified facts as the English ep 71: Cochrane review (Hemilä & Chalker, 2013): regular vitamin C had no effect on how often ordinary people
catch colds (29 trial comparisons, 11,306 people); taken daily, colds were ~8 % shorter in adults (14 % in children); started after symptoms began,
no consistent effect; in 5 trials with 598 marathon runners, skiers and soldiers on subarctic exercises, the cold risk was halved. NIH ODS: for most
people vitamin C supplements do not reduce the risk of getting a cold. Popular Science / Wikipedia: Linus Pauling (two Nobel Prizes) published
'Vitamin C and the Common Cold' in 1970 and the public went crazy for it (a rush of vitamin C sales).
~100 words, ~40-46 s spoken. Payoff ("नहीं!") in line 2 (~8 %), the 1970 origin at ~40 %, the twist at ~70 %.
