Topic: गाजर खाने से आँखों की रोशनी बढ़ती है? (same verified facts as the English ep 59)
Facts: Smithsonian Magazine (WWII propaganda campaign popularized the carrot myth): 1940, RAF night-fighter pilot John 'Cat's Eyes' Cunningham shot down enemy planes
with new airborne (A.I.) radar; the British government told newspapers the pilots ate lots of carrots; posters told the public carrots help you see in the blackout;
beta-carotene -> vitamin A; a shortage causes night blindness; vitamin A reverses poor vision from a deficiency but does not strengthen healthy eyes.
Scientific American 'Fact or Fiction?: Carrots Improve Your Vision': a cover-up for radar; vitamin A helps the eye see in low light; carrots help in settings with
real vitamin A deficiency (it names India); bingeing won't improve normal eyesight; most eye problems (genetics, aging, diabetes) aren't fixed by beta-carotene.
Live Science 'Can carrots give you night vision?': radar gave the edge, not the diet; carrots help someone lacking vitamin A, not people who already have enough.
UT Austin 'Think Twice' (2022): vitamin A is part of rhodopsin in the eye's rod cells; no improvement if you already have enough.
