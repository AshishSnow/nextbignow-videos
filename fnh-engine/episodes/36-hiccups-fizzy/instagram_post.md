Why do fizzy drinks give you hiccups? It's not the bubbles in your throat. It's your stomach.

A fizzy drink fills your stomach with gas, so it stretches fast, like a balloon, right under your diaphragm (the dome of muscle you breathe with).
The nerves there get irritated, the diaphragm jerks down, air rushes in, and your vocal cords snap shut. That snap is the "hic".
Most hiccups stop within a few minutes. If they last more than two days, see a doctor.
Weird bonus: babies hiccup in the womb, as early as nine weeks.
