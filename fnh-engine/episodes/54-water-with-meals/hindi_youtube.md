Topic: खाना खाते वक़्त पानी पीने से पेट का एसिड पतला होता है? (same verified facts as the English ep 54)
Facts: Mayo Clinic 'Water after meals: Does it disturb digestion?' (2025): water doesn't thin digestive fluids; water is part of stomach acid; people told to limit fluids (heart / kidney / liver disease) should follow that.
Hackensack Meridian Health (Dr Shamila Zawahir, 2022): no scientific evidence water interferes with digestion. StatPearls 'Physiology, Stomach' (NCBI/NIH): gastrin is released when the stomach stretches
and when its acid gets weaker (pH rises) and it raises acid production. India TV (Dr Manan Vora, 2026): only a very large amount at once may cause temporary bloating.
Spoken length target: ~40-45 s (≈ 105 words). Payoff (एसिड पानी से ही बनता है) at ~25 %.
